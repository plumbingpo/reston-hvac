Warning Signs for HVAC Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Weak airflow from the supply registers throughout the house
  • Grinding, squealing, buzzing, or banging you have not heard before
  • Vents blowing warm when you want cool, or cool when you want heat
  • Water pooling around the indoor equipment, often from a clogged condensate drain
  • An outside unit that buzzes but will not start
  • Short cycling, where the system starts and stops without ever reaching the setpoint
  • Higher energy bills with no improvement in comfort
  • A circuit breaker that keeps tripping when the system starts

HVAC Repair Prevention Tips

A little routine maintenance goes a long way toward avoiding a mid-season breakdown. Follow these tips from our experienced technicians:

  • Trim shrubs and clear leaves and grass away from the outdoor unit so the coil gets full airflow
  • Clear the condensate drain every spring so algae never backs it up and shuts the system down
  • Replace the air filter every one to three months to keep airflow strong across the blower and coil
  • Ease the strain in extreme weather by nudging the thermostat a few degrees toward the outdoor temperature
  • Book a professional tune-up in spring for cooling and in fall for heating
  • Take new noises seriously; a squealing motor bearing is far cheaper before it seizes
  • Leave every supply and return vent open and free of furniture or rugs
