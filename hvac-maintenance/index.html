The Benefits of Professional HVAC Maintenance

There is a short list of things homeowners can handle: changing filters on time, keeping the outdoor coil clear, leaving registers open, and checking the drain. The rest takes instruments, because the problems that leave you stranded give no visible warning. A capacitor that has lost 20 percent of its rating looks the same as a new one. Low charge shows up only in superheat and subcooling readings. Cracked heat exchangers, weak flame signals, marginal pressure switches, and amp draws creeping toward the breaker's limit are all invisible until measured, and all of them cost less to fix the season before they fail.

Once gas is involved, maintenance becomes a safety issue. A failing burner, flame proving circuit, vent, or draft problem can put combustion products where they do not belong, which is why those items get instrumented verification every year. Reston HVAC makes those safety checks the core of each fall visit, never an upsell. How to Prevent HVAC Maintenance Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly heating and cooling problems down the road. Start with these practical measures our team relies on:

  • Jot down any unusual sounds or run times, since they help your technician diagnose early
  • Clear the condensate drain early in cooling season, before algae has a chance to grow
  • Give the condenser room to breathe and rinse its coil lightly each spring
  • Swap the filter every one to three months, the most valuable habit a homeowner can build
  • If you have gas or other fuel-burning equipment, test your CO detectors every month
  • Book spring and fall visits in the shoulder seasons, before the rush and before the extremes
  • Resist closing vents in spare rooms; the system is designed with all registers open
